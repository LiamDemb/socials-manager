# End-to-end user journeys

## A. A single in four weeks

1. Campaigns → Create campaign → Single release. Select the known recording/release or create a pending local object. Reuse verified dates/URLs. For the supplied Better Man data, the artist ID is known from an owner URL but the recording remains provisional; a filename is not verification.
2. Set campaign window and primary outcome, for example first-week recording streams. Add pre-release confirmed pre-saves if a provider/report can measure them; show Needs source otherwise. Do not relabel artist saves as song saves. Date-only Spotify measurement remains UTC-day scoped.
3. Confirm existing channels, audience, assets/ready dates, capacity, budget and availability. The app already knows previous outcomes, source coverage, own post results and verified peer context; it does not ask for these again.
4. Generate an evidence bundle, supported tactic candidates and execution briefs. Review cards with dates/formats/support and gaps. The scheduler chooses feasible times from eligible timing history or explicit availability. Unsupported marketing tactics are withheld; operational source/link/asset tasks still make sense.
5. Open Why? to see the finding, observation versions, peers/own evidence, assumptions, limitations and source links. Open the compact Timing link to inspect the window choice separately. Browse matched references inside the activity. Inspiration is a creative reference, not proof of streams/pre-saves conversion.
6. Edit/deselect/move/add activities. Validate shared workload, dates/phase/dependencies/assets/budget and current evidence revisions. Approve atomically to populate Calendar. Creation/approval does not publish anything.
7. Calendar or Today → activity. Make the post from its brief, publish manually, record actual time/URL and complete. An uncompleted 5 pm item becomes overdue after 5 pm. Skipped/cancelled items need reasons. An experiment can span selected activities, with a preapproved comparison/window and later measurement review.
8. Sources imports/adapter observations match the verified recording/release/account. Recording stream flows, scoped pre-save snapshots and social observations stay distinct. Views show freshness and coverage; unavailable measurements are unknown.
9. Comparable new data creates reviewed change proposals. Accept/reject a diff; existing approved dates remain unchanged until acceptance. At campaign end, inspect results/experiments, record limitations and Repeat/Adapt/Stop/Need more evidence. Future campaigns retrieve this learning without asserting causation.

## B. Gain 100 followers in a month

Create Audience growth campaign, select the platform/account and one-month window, choose +100 from a compatible source-linked starting follower snapshot. There is no release selector. Link an existing longer-term artist outcome only when its scope/baseline/window exactly match; otherwise create a separate outcome contract.

The same evidence-backed generator proposes recurring work within capacity. Reach/profile visits may be supporting outcomes, never substituted for follower gain. Each activity has a purpose, practical brief, contextual references and qualified reasoning. Execute in one campaign calendar. Baseline/current compatible snapshots determine progress; completion counts do not.

Today reports artist movement and linked campaigns without adding the same shared follower contract twice. Being behind a linear pace prompts inspection of coverage/results and a supported adjustment proposal. It does not imply that adding a fixed number of posts guarantees 100 followers. Unknown baseline gives Unknown progress, not zero.

## C. Sell tickets for a show in six weeks

Create Live show campaign. Identify event, venue, show date/timezone, on-sale date and ticket destination where known. Set event-scoped net paid/issued ticket target, excluding refunds under the chosen reporting contract. Provide local audience, existing Instagram/Facebook/email channels, assets, availability and capacity. Reuse known event facts.

The app retrieves matched own/peer event patterns where permitted. Announcement/feed/event tasks, direct email invitation with an opted-in list, Stories/reminders and venue outreach can be candidates. It does not invent ticket conversion from likes or assume the band owns a usable email list. Missing ticket source becomes an operational setup task.

Approve dated activities, inspect event-matched references/reasons, execute and record actual timestamps. Latest cumulative net ticket snapshots update progress; successive totals are not summed. Page clicks and interest are distinct supporting measures. A sales/coverage change can propose a supported adjustment or source repair; no autonomous paid promotion or outreach.

## D. Day-to-day review

Today opens on artist-wide movement with compatible previous periods and data-through date, active campaign/outcome summaries, due/overdue work, imminent milestones and a few material alerts. Stale data is a repair action, not a flat trend. Every next action names its campaign, outcome or source.

Open an alert → its activity/campaign/source. Inspect/execute/reschedule with review, or drill into Evidence. Evidence maintains Findings / Observed data / Review context regardless of tab/route. From a finding, inspect its observations and sources then return to the same tab/filter. Plan-specific strategy stays in the campaign.

## E. Experiments

Create one when a useful uncertainty has a measurable comparison: for example whether performance footage or artwork is a better reach hypothesis at a common age. It links the real activities, outcome version, primary variable, comparison, windows and known confounders. It can cross campaigns only with explicit reviewed links; no duplicate calendar items.

Before: draft/approve protocol and baseline; disclose non-randomised comparison limits. During: execute linked work, preserve actual times and record competing exposure. Moving/format-changing approved work flags the protocol. After: wait for mature compatible observations, inspect data/limits and record Repeat/Adapt/Stop/Need more evidence. Inconclusive is valid. The result updates versioned findings/learning for future retrieval, not an unexplained global AI belief or guaranteed causal coefficient.

## F. Review context, previously Review Labels

A source caption may ambiguously mention a forthcoming song, an existing video, two artists or an uncertain date. Rules/audited model proposes separate labels for intent, format, campaign phase, promoted object and CTA, with evidence spans. Only uncertain/conflicting/high-impact cases need attention.

Evidence → Review context → original text/source + proposed interpretation → confirm/correct/leave unknown → save new interpretation version. No raw caption is rewritten. Dependent findings/proposals become stale/recomputed; approved activities are flagged for inspection, not deleted. This improves contextual retrieval/comparability and may provide permitted labelled evaluation examples. It does not automatically authorise model training.

## G. Evidence, peers, sources and inspiration

Evidence links what was observed to what was derived, with age/window, metric/scope, sample, method and limitations. External source drill-down shows page name/link, publication date when available, retrieved date, extracted information and influence. A source capability article is not marketing-effect evidence.

Peers starts with a small curated cohort: verify identity, inspect genre/location/scale/cadence and observable behaviour, include/exclude/pin. Unresolved peers cannot drive recommendations. Public interaction counts do not expose private reach, tickets, streams or follower-online activity. Cohort selection/exclusions are versioned to avoid silently selecting only winners.

Sources is the operational home: preview/import/resolve/commit/undo, freshness/coverage/capability and connector recovery. Keep it easy to use. Inspiration remains global browsing plus activity/outcome context. Save/attach/reject signals creative preference, not measured performance. Reference reuse respects original ownership; link rather than copying media by default.

## H. AI-assisted creation and Ask

The user supplies a structured objective and resources. The app retrieves eligible evidence, derives findings/ranks tactics, lets a bounded model extract context/synthesise a brief, and deterministically validates the proposed schedule and scope. Why? makes the chain inspectable. No generic chat is required to build a campaign.

Ask uses selected campaign/activity/finding scope or clearly resets to artist scope. It retrieves permitted evidence and returns concise citations, limitations and navigation. Requested edits become a validated proposal/diff. It cannot secretly mutate records, run arbitrary tools, publish, browse private accounts or bypass source-use restrictions. Model unavailable means a visible fallback, while the rest of the application works.
